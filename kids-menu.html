7 Brew Kids Menu

Looking for a kid-friendly drink for your next drive-thru run? 7 Brew offers a delightful range of caffeine-free, smaller-portioned drinks specifically crafted for kids, including smoothies, shakes, lemonades, and custom sparkling sodas.

Last updated: August 2, 2026 | Reviewed by 7BrewGuide Editorial Team

Kid-Friendly Drink Categories

Real Fruit Smoothies (12 oz)

Thick, creamy, and blended with real fruit purees. Available flavors include Strawberry, Peach, Mango, and Wildberry.

Creamy Shakes (12 oz)

Rich and hand-blended vanilla ice cream shakes. Choose from Vanilla, Chocolate, Caramel, or Cookies & Cream.

Sweet & Tart Lemonades (12 oz)

Classic ice-cold lemonades customized with sweet syrup additions like strawberry or blue raspberry.

Custom 7 Fizz Sparkling Sodas (12 oz)

Crisp carbonated water sweetened with your choice of kid-friendly fruit syrups (like cherry or raspberry) for a custom caffeine-free soda.

Frequently Asked Questions

What size are kids drinks at 7 Brew?

Kids size drinks at 7 Brew are served in a smaller, kid-friendly 12 oz cup size.

Are 7 Brew kids drinks caffeine-free?

Yes. The kids menu includes naturally caffeine-free drinks like real fruit smoothies, milkshakes, lemonades, and sparkling fruit sodas (7 Fizz).
